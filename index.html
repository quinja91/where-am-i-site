What it is

A photograph, a world of possibilities, and ninety seconds. You name a city; the score is how far off you were. Ten points inside 25 km, four for the right country, one for the right continent — so a wild guess in roughly the right part of the world still counts for something.

The photographs come from players. Not Street View, not stock imagery — real pictures taken on real phones, in places people actually were. Uploads are anonymous and reviewed before they enter the pool.

Why it’s hard

Difficulty isn’t about whether you have heard of the city. A beach in Barcelona is a place everyone can name and nobody can place: sand, sea and sky look the same in eighty countries. A back street in a town you have never heard of can be far easier, if the signage, the road markings or the light give it away.

So the game sorts photographs by how much they give away, not by how famous the answer is — and the harder buckets pay more.
